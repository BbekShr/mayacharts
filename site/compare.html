Not measured here

The tables above cover twelve common charts, chosen by us. These are the places where the other libraries are likely ahead, and none of them is scored:

  • Chart types. ECharts and Plotly ship many more, such as candlestick, gauge, radar, maps and 3D. mayaCharts has no pie chart, by design.
  • Drawing every point. Chart.js and ECharts draw to canvas, and Plotly can use WebGL, so they can put millions of marks on screen. mayaCharts stays SVG and reduces large data before drawing it (downsampling and binning).
  • Interaction depth. Brushing linked charts, data zoom sliders, toolbars and image export are built into some rivals.
  • Custom code. Rivals accept functions for formatters, tooltips and drawing. mayaCharts takes plain JSON only, so some layouts are not possible.
  • Maturity. The rivals have years of users, plugins, answers and docs. mayaCharts is new.
