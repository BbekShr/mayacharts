## What is worth adding next (ranked)

1. **Fix the label blink on streamed updates** (element engineer). Value labels and end labels vanish for part of every step on the bar race and live line. This is what keeps the motion tiles from looking finished. Element bundle has 25 B of headroom.
2. **`frame: "<field>"`** opt-in playback: the element steps through a field with a Play button. Folds the 21 to 23 line host loop into one spec line. About +200 B index, +500 B element and global. Needs a budget decision.
3. **100% stack** (`stack: "percent"`), about 100 B core. Every rival ships it.
4. **Donut and funnel** in a new opt-in `mayacharts/stat` module (about 400 B each, own budget, core untouched). Box plot and histogram after them.
5. Per-field `aggregate` (about 50 B) and `cumulative: true` (about 70 B) remove the remaining host data shaping in the gallery.
6. Sankey link gradients and whole-path hover: flow module has 39 B left.

Rejected: a `rank: 10` shorthand for horizontal+sort+limit+labels (four meanings in one word), auto `titles` from field names (English only, "MonthMs"), changing any default. Drill: already shown on hbar, treemap, sunburst and sankey, and a drilled bar already zooms into its children; extending drill to waterfall, heatmap and radial is backlog.
