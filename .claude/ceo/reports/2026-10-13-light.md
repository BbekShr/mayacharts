## First principles

1. What did we learn? A chart nested in a mark (facet panel, table row) repeats categories and keys, so every lookup and every animation container id needs each nesting level in it. Facet was scoped; expand was not, in two places.
2. Foundations: identity has to match what the reader sees as the same thing. A trail segment is "March to April", not "the second newest"; a sub-chart belongs to its row, not to "the open slot".
3. More: any future sparkline-in-cell or drill preview takes the same `data-row` style scope. The pager in the hits group is the same class of bug (unkeyed, so it jumps).
4. Limit: a capped trail still steps `stroke-opacity` per position as it rolls (an attribute, so it jumps). Backlogged.
