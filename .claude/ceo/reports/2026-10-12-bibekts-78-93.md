## Review rounds after M5

- Security: no critical or high. Fixed: hostile and fuzz coverage for list and every new option, `drillTo` was fallback, theme registry re-validated on read, `back` forbids the same options as `expand`, a nesting cap (24 levels, 100k nodes) so hostile JSON throws a spec error, not a stack overflow.
- Design critic: 1 high (list rank collided with the headline after count-up), 9 medium, 7 low; all fixed except the ones listed under ceilings.
- Motion critic: 3 high (ghosts flashed black, scrubbing froze bars, facet and new drills did not zoom), 3 medium, 1 low; fixed except the ceilings below.

## Ceilings (NON-FEATURES)

WebKit facet panels jump instead of sliding (no transform on nested svg); line drillTo pops in (no zoom box); an opened list move overlaps its evidence for about 120 ms; two thin drilled marimekko columns can overprint their names; waterfall axis shortening is positive running totals only.
