## The load jerk

Four causes, all fixed:

1. Scale page (site): after each tile's entrance, `site/scale.ts` narrowed the chart by 8 px and restored it to time a resize. Two instant redraws per tile, about 1.5 s after it appeared (viewBox 576, 568, 576). The nudge is gone; the Resize readout fills on a real window resize (cf7d937).
2. Element: a resize during the first draw (a scrollbar appearing, a font swap, a grid settling) cancelled the entrance, so bars snapped while axes kept fading. A resize frame now waits until the entrance has played (ba4e80e). Running mark animations 80 ms after a resize at 60 ms: 16 to 17 with the fix, 3 to 4 without, in chromium, webkit and firefox.
3. Element: a page that draws many charts in one task (the gallery, any dashboard) started each WebKit animation clock at `animate()`, so the top charts were 95% through their entrance at the first visible frame. Entrances now restart at their first painted frame, and Safari's late table insert waits 1.6 s instead of 0 (420e791). WebKit measured 171 to 186 ms into the entrance before, 46 to 55 ms after (two probe frames).
4. Scale page layout shift: the notes section painted and was pushed off screen when the tiles were appended. CLS 0.20 to about 0 (0fe3b8e).

Also from the motion critic: stacked, percent and marimekko columns grow as one instead of floating slivers; waterfall start and total bars and boxplot dots keep the stagger (only funnel connectors wait); wipe and bloom intros run above the 1500-mark cap (Scale line-8 and area had no entrance); rules fade in with the axes; no pop overshoot on sized circles; no hover border scaling through a drill zoom.
