## First principles

1. Arcs tween in angle space, never by morphing `d`. CSS interpolates an A command's endpoints along the chord with a fixed radius, so the centre drifts; a pathLength circle's dash is the angle as one number. Applies to every radial progress mark.
2. Bands need lightness steps, not hues at equal alpha: different hues at the same transparency land within about 1.05:1 of each other.
3. A drop-until-it-fits layout needs a final shrink step for the item it never drops, or fixed items collide.
4. A count-up is a second encoding of the value; it must start where the mark starts.
