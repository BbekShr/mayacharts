# Non-Features

mayaCharts stays small so it stays maintained. These are deliberate, permanent (or until-proven-necessary) exclusions, and issues requesting them will be closed with a link here.

## Permanently excluded

- No sankey, network/graph, 3D, geo/maps (beyond hexmap), gantt, or chord in core. These may become separate optional packages via `mayacharts/` modules; core stays small.
- No plugin system. A plugin API is an unbounded support surface.
- No Canvas or WebGL renderer. SVG is accessible, SSR-safe, and fast enough up to several thousand marks; revisited only when a real user hits the ceiling.
- No framework wrappers (React/Vue/Svelte/Angular). `<maya-chart>` is a standard Custom Element that works in all of them without adaptation.
- No function-valued options, including tick/label formatter callbacks. The spec is plain JSON so it can be generated by LLMs, stored, diffed, hashed and rendered on a server. Use the `format` field with presets or Intl options.
- No runtime dependencies.
- No hand-rolled animation engine, tooltip positioning engine or theme system. These come from the browser (Web Animations API, CSS Anchor Positioning + Popover, CSS custom properties).
- No CommonJS build. ESM only (except for one IIFE in dist/maya.global.js for paste-anywhere use).
- No relicensing: the core is MIT, forever.

## Known ceilings

- **List rows**: moves past the tile height are not drawn; body wrap and clip widths are estimates (about 6.5 px a body character, 8 px a headline character), right for Latin text at 12 to 14 px.
- **List evidence format**: evidence values use the move's own format unless the evidence row sets one; the evidence scale is local to each move and never compares across moves.
- **Table strips**: each strip square is a mark, so a row adds `last` keyboard stops.
- **Table without a first bar**: with `bars` "none" on the first measure, a row's tooltip comes from the row label.
- **Waterfall axis**: when every running total is positive and their spread is under half the highest, the value axis starts one spread below the lowest and the total bars break at the axis; the 2:1 trigger is fixed, `yDomain` overrides it.
- **Narrow table**: columns that do not fit are dropped from the right, and an 18 px strip at the header's end says "+n".
- **Drilled marimekko**: segments are tints of the column's colour (steps repeat after 8 siblings); the series legend still shows the plain slot colours.
- **Line and area was**: the ghost is a dashed line per series, drawn under the line; a gap in `was` breaks it.
- **Facet panels**: at least 160 x 110 px each (past that the grid scrolls) and sparklines under 240 x 150 px. Panels share the hidden data table of the whole chart, which sums over the facets. A line or area thinned past its point budget thins each panel on its own, so the kept x values may differ between panels. Sub-charts (`back`, `expand`, `drillTo`) and facet panels cannot reference `params`. Drilling into or out of a facet panel crossfades; the panel box does not zoom.
- **Params**: at most 4 sliders, one `scale` entry, and percent mode only; `scale` multiplies every `y` field of the matching rows. `{ param }` refs work in colorBy `target` and `warn` and rules `y`, `y2`, `x` and band ends, not in `yDomain`, slopes or other numbers. The warn-below-target check uses the params' spec values.
- **Trails**: one position per name and frame (the name's first row in that frame).

- Theme `heroSize` and `labelSize` cap the fitted kpi, gauge and hexmap sizes in px only; `rem`, `em` and `calc()` values do not cap (the fit stands).
- Tints (`tint`, `data-lt`) only mix toward the ink, so a step never loses contrast on the page; there is no lighter-than-the-series step in light mode.

- **Weave threads**: 8, or 20 with a group `colorBy`. Groups share the 8 palette slots and `tint` tells threads apart; past 20, filter or group first.
- **Radar**: no area fill (overlapping series would hide each other); category names thin by collision.
- **Label ink tokens**: `labelInk` and `labelInkOnFill` reach only browsers without relative colours; CSS cannot choose between two author colours by a channel of the fill, so modern browsers use near-black and white by luminance.
- **kpi back face**: the back face shares the front's view (a toggled kpi's `view.measure` also indexes a `y` array on the back) and its rows (the active frame's), and the shell holds one face at a time, so turning the card re-renders. A back face cannot have its own `data`, `back` or kpi type.
- **Table rows**: `scroll` draws at most 1000 rows and `page` stops adding past 1000; filter or aggregate beyond that. The search matches substrings of x and `columns`, ignoring case, with no wildcards.
- **Table expand**: one row open at a time, and its sub-chart has no state of its own (no drill, zoom, frame, paging or filter). A row whose rows miss a field the sub-chart needs stays shut and the reason is in `Parts.warnings`.
- **Waterfall drill**: a drilled step is rebased by summing the rows before it, whatever `aggregate` says.
- **Heatmap totals**: the Total row and column combine the rows of visible series over the drawn columns (an Other column holds the rest); they are not part of the colour ramp.
- **Series drill**: `seriesPath` and `path` do not combine on one chart; drill the categories or the series. A line's `drillTo` opens one series at a time.
- **Chord drill**: three levels at most; the drill splits one target, the sources never split.
- **Table strip cells**: a strip reads flat rows, one per row label and period; array-valued cells are not supported.
- **List**: the moves keep data order (no sort or limit) and one move's evidence shares one scale.
- **Stack modes**: a `stack` array switches between whole-chart modes; `was`, a ratio aggregate and (on bar) `stream` are refused for the whole array, even when one mode would allow them.
- **Gauge ring text**: on a 270 or 360 dial the change row inside the ring is cut to the chord at its height (5.8 px a character, estimated, not measured); a long status plus change line shows an ellipsis on a small ring.

- **Compact tile on SSR**: `data-compact` (host height under 200 px, which clamps the title to a line and hides the subtitle) is set by the element, so a server-rendered tile shows the full title and subtitle until hydration.
- **Value magnitude**: a numeric field (y, y2, size, was, colorBy, scatter x) must stay within 1e300 in absolute value; past it validation fails with the field's numeric error and the hint "Scale the value first". Sums of values that large overflow the scale and layout arithmetic to NaN, so the ceiling is checked per value, not per total (two 1e300 values still sum safely).
- **Tooltip rows**: a tooltip shows at most 12 rows (a `+N` row counts the rest); a category with more series shows a window around the hovered one. Units: ArrowUp and ArrowDown do nothing because each dot is its own group, and during a form flight the pointer picks dots by where they will land. A boxplot outlier has no category group, so ArrowLeft and ArrowRight from it jump to the first box.
- **Module row-pass cache**: sunburst, treemap, sankey, chord, boxplot and beeswarm cache their row pass by data array identity, length, first row and last row, so a middle row edited in place needs a new array.
- **Dense bars**: a horizontal bar chart with too many categories for one label per 14 px of plot height, no `limit` and a nominal x rolls the smallest into Other. The height is estimated before layout, and month names in data order roll up like any other names (set `limit` or `xType: "time"` to avoid it). Vertical bars keep thinning their labels.
- **was.by grouping**: the long-form `was` pivot groups rows by (x, series) only, so a drill path with the same x under two parents merges, and other fields come from the first row of each group.
- **Treemap tints and notes**: tiles of one hue (a drilled branch, a nested treemap's siblings) step through 8 lightnesses by size rank, so more than 8 tiles repeat them. `annotate { x, text }` notes label the first visible series' mark at x, not stacked bars, and a note replaces that bar's value label. A nested treemap's parents get a header strip only when they are at least 36 by 34 px.
- **Annotations**: `annotate` labels the max, min, first and last point per series once each (the first of equal values), skips the Other roll-up and stacked bars, and skips "last" on a line that has end labels. A label with no room outside its bar goes inside the bar end, or is dropped when that does not fit either.
- **Slope rules**: a scatter `{ slope }` rule is a straight line, so it is drawn only when both axes are linear or both logarithmic; with one log axis it is skipped. Its label (the value after it, else the label alone, else the value alone) tries ten spots along the line, 10 px off it on either side, and takes the one covering the fewest points, using a width guess of 6.5 px per character, so a very wide font can still touch a point.
- **Dashed lines**: the dash is set in pathLength units from the polyline's length at draw time (6 px on, 4 px off), so it is exact after a render and approximate while a path morphs between two lengths.
- **Beeswarm density edges**: density counts use a binary search over each band's sorted values, so a value exactly on a column edge can land one column over from the pixel computation.
- **Element legend pre-pass**: the first-paint legend of a multi-series chart is placed from the series in the first 1000 rows; a series that first appears later costs one extra draw. `play` counts frames with one pass over `data` on each Play click, and drill's spec-event reducer filters rows once per spec replacement while drilled; neither runs on any other interaction.
- **Beeswarm density cells**: past 10000 points beeswarm merges series and ignores `name` (no per-series violins), and bins are about 6 px wide along the value axis, at most 10000 cells in total.
- **Sankey and chord nodes**: more than 10000 distinct nodes throws `too-many-marks` while the rows are read, because the layout after it is quadratic.
- **Scale page**: the demo's Scale page keeps one shared table of row objects (about 95 MB per million rows in Chromium), and its draw timings are single runs on the visitor's machine, not benchmarks.
- **Row count**: a million rows is the tested promise. With nine-field rows every type stays under a second to draw and a quarter second to resize up to about 2 million; a bar with one category per row is the slow case (about 3 s at 2 million before it rolls up into Other). Past that the browser's memory for the row objects, not the chart, is the limit. Columnar input is not offered.
- **Dense charts skip hover motion**: above 500 marks the svg carries `data-still` and every CSS transition (hover dim, crosshair and band glide) is off, because repainting the whole svg each frame of a sweep ran at about 8 fps at 5000 bars. Entrance and update animations (WAAPI) are unaffected.
- **Dense dim is per paint**: dense charts (`svg[data-still]`, over 500 marks) dim through `fill-opacity` and `stroke-opacity` instead of `opacity`, because WebKit paints each opacity-dimmed mark as its own group (about 555 ms a hover frame at 5000 marks). Where a mark's stroke overlaps its fill the dim is a shade darker than a group dim. Lines with hidden points (`[data-pt]`) keep the `opacity` dim.
- **Sankey node count**: link keys are numbers (`source * 2^26 + target`), so a sankey stops at about 67 million nodes.
- **Builder paste size**: the demo site's chart builder previews at most 1 MB, 10,000 rows (the `too-many-marks` cap) and 50 columns of pasted data, and its copied code inlines at most 60 rows (the first 20 when there are more). The library itself has no such limit; load the full data in your own code.
- **Radial bar centre label**: the centre label and the shared year hide below about 170 px wide, and a year-first locale keeps full names.
- **Radial stacked tip totals of means**: the radial centre total shows only for sum and count (a sum of per-category means is not a total). The tip total of a stacked radial bar is still the sum of its segments, so with `aggregate: "mean"` or another non-additive one it reads as a sum of means. Use one series, or sum, for an honest tip.
- **Render batching**: property sets in one task coalesce into one render (microtask), but sets in separate tasks each render, so a host that updates faster than 60 Hz is not capped at one render per frame. Resizes still wait for a frame.
- **Scatter pointer pick**: scatter has no hit circles; the tooltip and click pick the nearest point centre within 12 px by a linear scan, so a pick costs O(points) per pointer move.
- **Thinned sparkline and ridgeline**: kpi (one hover point per 4 px) and ridgeline (one per 6 px of plot width) keep the same largest-triangle-three-buckets pick as a downsampled time axis (each series' minimum and maximum and every gap edge included), so a click or hover lands on a kept category only, not every row. When the kept set is still over the target it is thinned evenly, which can drop a gap edge or a peak in the densest cases.
- **Per-row marks stay capped**: beeswarm (a dot per row) and parallel (a line and a dot per measure per row, so about 2500 rows) throw `too-many-marks` at 10000 marks. Drawing less would mean binning, which is a different chart; use scatter, `limit` or aggregate the rows first.
- **Orbit label placement**: an orbit name rides its planet, counter-turned so it stays upright and radially outward of the planet. At most the 6 largest planets are named, largest first, placed at the starting angle; a name that would touch the sun, another planet, an earlier name or leave the plot there is dropped (the rest answer to hover). Planets turn at different speeds, so a planet or its trail can pass across another name for a moment.
- **Units cap**: `units` draws at most 1500 dots (`too-many-marks` above that), the number the form flight can animate; the swarm form checks only the 50 nearest placed dots, so a heavy tie can overlap, and it shrinks its dots (not the other forms') to fit its band. Aggregate or use beeswarm or scatter for more rows.
- **Memory ghost bars are plain bars only**: `was` draws ghost bars on grouped or single bars. It is not available with `stack`, including a `stack` array with a grouped mode (a ghost per segment would hide the stack); with a `y` array it must be keyed by y field (the previous value belongs to one measure). An object `was` with a string `by` is always the long form, so a measure named "by" cannot be keyed. The description names only the 2 largest relative moves; the data table has every value. The ghost is a dashed rect, so its sides show faintly down to the baseline as well as its top edge.
- **Orbit speed has 5 buckets**: an orbit planet's speed is its growth quantised to 5 durations (a theme rule on `data-v`), not a continuous rate. The planets are the hover targets (no hit shapes). They turn all the time and stop where they are under a pointer, keyboard focus or an active planet (a short entrance sweep on first draw), never with `animate: false` or reduced motion; the static trail arc carries the growth in a still. With negative growth values the sun shows the sum of absolute values (gross, not net, titled "Gross"), so planet areas add up to it; negative planets are drawn paler.
- **Constellation caps**: a constellation fails above 2000 rows (the nearest-neighbour search is O(n^2), about 185 ms at 2000) and 12 measures. It does not mirror in right-to-left pages (the library keeps `.maya-svg{direction:ltr}` and a PCA layout has no reading order), draws no hit shapes (the 12 px nearest-star pick of scatter would need `constellation` added to the element's pick list), and lights a hovered star's 3 nearest through each star's `data-a`, not by drawing their lines. Five names at most are placed greedily (above, below, right, then left of the star) and the hint line is clipped to the plot width, not wrapped.
- **Late data table**: the hidden data table is inserted when the browser is next idle after a render (2 s at most), and only the latest one, so a screen reader sees it a moment after the marks. A server-rendered table stays in place until then.
- **Reference lines**: `rules` draws at most 4 items (value lines, category lines, value bands, scatter slopes) at values up to 1e15 in size, with labels of at most 40 characters. A rule label tries a few spots along its line inside the plot after value labels and annotations, and is dropped when none is free; it avoids labels, not marks (a bubble under it keeps the label's halo). A rule on a downsampled time axis averages the kept points only.
- **Y-domain origin**: value axes (lines included) are 0-anchored. Negative values are supported; set `yDomain` to override the origin.
- **Ramp depth**: 10-step sequential ramp for colorBy numeric fields (not user-tunable). Only steps 8 and 9 reach 3:1 as fill (3:1 needs about 80% accent, which leaves no range), so heatmap cells and hexes carry a full-accent outline (3.9:1 light, 4.8:1 dark) as the WCAG 1.4.11 mark; the ramp itself stays a hint.
- **Text width is estimated**: core has no text measurement (it runs in Node), so every fit check counts 0.6 em per code point and 1 em per East-Asian-wide one; a font much wider or narrower than the default can still clip or leave room.
- **Label truncation**: a category axis with 8 or fewer labels, or slots of 60 px or more, is never thinned, each label is clipped to its slot with an ellipsis (full text in a title); longer axes are thinned. Text is measured by counting East-Asian-wide characters at 1 em, not rendered width (no DOM measurement).
- **Time axis scope**: line, area and vertical bar only, on the bottom axis. UTC only (no time zones or DST), no fiscal or ISO-week calendars, no sub-second ticks; a bare year like "2024" is a category. Numbers become epoch ms only with `xType: "time"`.
- **Table width**: a narrow table drops its rightmost measure columns (no horizontal scroll); a column needs its widest value plus 16 px.
- **Grouped bar labels**: a label wider than its bar is drawn above it and dropped only if it would overprint another label; no staggering to a second row. A waterfall step label wider than its column is dropped, but the opening and closing totals hug their outer edge and stay.
- **Colours past 8 series**: a `colors` map takes any number of names, but there are 8 palette slots, so two series 8 apart in first-appearance order share a slot and the later key's colour.
- **Empty-state wrap**: `text.noData` wraps by the estimated text width and is not cut to the box height, so a very long message on a very short tile can still overflow vertically.
- **Thinned line gaps**: on a downsampled time line the median step is the thinning's, so only a hole of about 6 mean steps (about 6 px) breaks the line; smaller real gaps do not show.
- **Time line gaps**: a line breaks where the gap between readings exceeds 5 times the series' median step (fixed factor); use null rows for gaps it should not guess.
- **Time bar width**: bars on a time axis take 0.8 of the smallest gap between dates, so one close pair makes every bar thin.
- **Time tick labels**: a `format` entry for x replaces the per-unit tick labels entirely.
- **Downsampling**: line and area (a time axis past one point per 2 px of plot width, a category axis past 1000 categories), at most 1000 points (fewer with many series; more than about 100 series or many gaps can exceed it). The width term moves in steps of 50 points so a resize reuses the cached reduction. LTTB runs per series over all series, hidden ones included. A thinned line breaks where kept points sit more than 6 mean steps apart (about 6 px), not at 5 times the median (thinning widens every step), so a real gap shows but one under a few pixels is bridged; a null still breaks it. A category axis is thinned over the category index, so an unordered category list gets a meaningless "trend" kept; sort or aggregate first. Bars on a time axis are not reduced and stop at 10000 marks.
- **Point budget width**: the plot width the downsampling target uses is estimated as chart width minus 56 px, because shape runs before layout fixes the margins; a very wide y axis keeps a few points more than one per 2 px.
- **Scatter hits**: scatter draws no hit elements; the element picks the nearest mark centre within 12 px, so a dense plot picks by distance, not by drawn circle.
- **Density cells**: scatter past 10000 visible points draws square cells (not hexagons), merges series into one ramp, drops `size` and `name`, and scales colour by the square root of the count against the visible maximum.
- **Format templates**: one `{value}` or `{value:<preset>}` per template, at most 80 characters; no other placeholders such as the series or category name.
- **Drill engine**: `drill: true` + `path` for navigation. No custom drill templates, custom breadcrumb shapes, or drilling by a measure-derived category (use `path` and `aggregate`).
- **Zoom keys are coarse**: with focus on the chart or a mark, `+` or `=` halves the zoom window about the active mark (else the middle) and `-` doubles it, reset at the full range; there is no arrow-key pan and no way to type an exact window (hosts can set `el.view.window`).
- **No pinch or wheel zoom**: `zoom: true` on line/area/scatter enables drag-to-brush and `+` / `-` keys; Reset chip, double-click and Escape restore. Pinch gestures and scroll wheel are not captured.
- **Drill and select share fixed gestures**: with both on, a click or Enter selects and a double-click or Shift+Enter drills (or the pinned tooltip's "Drill into" button). The split is fixed; there is no option to swap the gestures. A double-click leaves the selection as it was before it.
- **Percent stacks show shares only**: with `stack: "percent"` labels, tooltips and the data table show each segment's share; the raw value is not carried. Chart the same data with `stack: true` to read amounts.
- **Playback is minimal**: the frame Play button steps every 900 ms (fixed, no speed control) and stops at the last frame. It stops when the spec object changes or the element disconnects, but not when scrolled out of view, and Arrow keys on the button do not step frames (use `el.view = { frame }`).
- **Frame size scale**: with `frame` and `size`, the size scale reads the raw rows of every frame (hidden series and windows included) so dots hold their size between frames.
- **Frame axes**: with `frame`, every render shapes every frame once to fix the value axes (cost grows with frames times rows, so more than 200 distinct frames fail with too-many-marks). Frame values match as strings, so 1 and "1" are one frame. Category axes are not shared: a category that is missing from a frame drops out of it, and on a time axis the dates shown follow the frame. Rows whose frame value is null belong to no frame.
- **Stack labels show segments**: stacked bar labels display the segment value, not the total. Column labels in a heatmap show the cell value.
- **Scatter keyboard order**: keyboard navigation follows mark draw order, not spatial proximity.
- **10000-mark hard cap**: `MAX_MARKS = 10000`. Above it, waterfalls, dumbbells, parallel charts and module charts fail with `too-many-marks` (the message gives the count, the cap and the row count), suggesting `limit` or `aggregate`; bars roll up into Other, lines and kpi thin, scatters bin and tables draw the rows that fit instead.
- **Auto roll-up for bars**: a bar with more categories than fit (past `MAX_MARKS` categories times series) and no `limit` keeps the top N by total, N = 10 per 40 px of plot width, and sums the rest into Other, with a warning in `Parts.warnings`. It never applies to a waterfall (a running total cannot lose steps), to a bar on a time axis (the order is the point), or to hidden series (the count uses every series, so a legend toggle never changes N). The automatic cut ranks by absolute total, so a -1,000,000 bar is kept and is not hidden in Other; an explicit `limit: N` ranks by signed total. Otherwise its output is the same as `limit: N`. A clipped Other bar prints its total at the break (without `labels`, too). The Other bucket merges the rolled-up categories' aggregates (a mean by its counts), so a new N after a resize is a selection, not a row pass.
- **Row-pass cache**: shape, validation, scatter's columns and the table's groups cache their row pass per data array (a `WeakMap` keyed by identity, 256 entries per array, plus the fields they read and the array length), so a resize, legend toggle or zoom does not walk the rows again. The key also holds the array's first and last row, so a push, a shift or a replaced last row is noticed; a host that edits any other row in place sees stale charts: pass a new array (`data = [...data]`). The cache holds typed columns and aggregates for as long as the array lives (about 50 MB at 1M rows). Treemap and sunburst (the tree), sankey and chord (the graph), boxplot and beeswarm (the scan) use the same memo.
- **Kpi thins in shape**: a kpi over 1000 categories is thinned in shape to its sparkline budget plus the last two live values, so its description and data table list the kept points, not every row; the headline and delta are from the full series.
- **Table sorts every render**: a sorted or limited table sorts every category on each render (only the grouping is cached). A million categories take about a second to sort; the table draws only the rows that fit its height. Its hidden data table is built from the shaped cells (first 1000).
- **US-only hexmap**: hexmap covers the 50 states plus DC and PR; world maps are out of scope.
- **Sankey without crossing minimisation**: sankey forces nodes per level without reordering to minimise link crossings (fast, deterministic, sufficient for publication).
- **UTC dates**: date presets format in `timeZone: "UTC"` so server and client agree; pass Intl options with a `timeZone` for local time.
- **SSR width**: `renderShell` lays out at `width` x `height` (default 640 x 320); pass the container's size or the first client paint re-lays out visibly.
- **Colour slots by first appearance**: a series keeps its palette slot while row order is stable; reordering rows can move colours (use `colors: { value: color }` to pin them). Nine or more series wrap the 8 slots. Path types (treemap, sunburst, sankey, chord) take slots from all rows before drill, so a name keeps its colour through drill, but a host filter that drops names can still move array slots; a `colors` map never moves. Under a map a name shares its slot (modulo 8) with any name 8 slots away.
- **Animation cap**: updates with more than 1500 marks are drawn without animation.
- **Path morph browsers**: line and area updates morph only where the browser interpolates CSS `d` (Chromium, Firefox) and the path keeps its command sequence; Safari, and paths that gain or lose points, crossfade instead.
- **Gradient ids in standalone SVG**: area fills reference `#maya-a0`..`#maya-a7`. Several `render()` SVGs inlined in one page share those ids, so a chart with custom `colors` may show another chart's gradient colours (the element's shadow root scopes them; the flat fallback tint is used if the reference fails).
- **Stacked bar labels**: a positive stacked segment too short for its value drops the label rather than printing it over its neighbour; a negative segment keeps its label below the bar, which assumes one negative series per column.
- **Bar width**: bars stop growing at 72 px and sit centred in their slot.
- **Flow node labels**: node labels that would collide with a larger node's label are dropped (the tooltip still names them).
- **Radial tip values**: a bar's total shows at its tip only when it fits (wedge arc of 11 px or more, 40 categories or fewer); otherwise it is in the tooltip.
- **Marimekko thin columns**: a column too thin for any name (under 12 px) gets a six-character label beside it, and a neighbour's label can touch it.
- **Gauge percent change**: a percent-measure gauge with `was` shows the change in points only; the relative percent is left out because it clips "vs ..." inside the ring.
- **Sunburst hairlines**: an arc under 2 px along its middle is not drawn, nor its children; its rows stay in the data table.
- **Sunburst slivers**: once a slice would be under 4 px across, it and its smaller siblings draw as one grey "Other (n)" slice that does not drill; its rows are only in the data table. Slices under 2 px of outer arc are not drawn, nor their children. Rotated names skip the label overlap scan; each is fitted to its own ring instead. `minAngle` sets the fold threshold in degrees instead (0 folds nothing, though arcs under 2 px stay undrawn).
- **Entrance**: the first draw animates once; server-rendered charts never replay it.
- **Measure toggle up to 4 options**: a `y` array works with more measures, but the sliding indicator is styled for 2 to 4.
- **kpi text fit is estimated**: titles and comparison lines are cut at about 6.6 px per character of 12 px text, not measured per tile, so a narrow percent tile can cut its label ("vs tar…") and wide glyphs or CJK text can still overflow.
- **kpi in a short box**: the sparkline needs about 28 px of free height; with a target bullet and too little room, the bullet stays and the sparkline is dropped.
- **kpi comparisons are fixed**: the change lines compare with the previous period, with `was` (for example the same period last year) and with the `colorBy` target. Other baselines are not supported.
- **Box plot scope**: vertical boxes only; no notches, violins or mean marker; every row is drawn as a dot only when a box holds 30 rows or fewer (outliers always); quartiles use linear interpolation (R-7, Excel QUARTILE.INC).
- **Funnel scope**: one measure, so no period compare (use dumbbell for two periods); stages keep data order, with no sort or limit; stages enter with a 0.6 scale pop from their centre, not from zero width.
- **Box median label**: one placement for every box, never mixed: right of the box when every label fits before the next box, else above the median inside the box, else below it, when every box is tall enough, else none (the tooltip and table still carry it). Never above the upper cap, which would read as the maximum. `labels: false` drops them.
- **Funnel step labels**: the step % between bands is dropped when the gap between bands is under 12 px (the tooltip still carries it).
- **One y2 line**: `y2` takes one field, aggregated per category across every series, on a 0-anchored right axis.
- **Dumbbell sort**: `sort` orders categories by the total of both values, not by the gap between them.
- **Ridgeline scale**: rows share one value scale from zero with no per-row axis; peaks overlap the row above by at most 40%. A scale from the data minimum does not help when rows differ in magnitude, and per-row scaling would exaggerate small movements.
- **Beeswarm height**: a swarm taller than its row is clamped to the row, so overflow points overlap.
- **Parallel axes**: every axis runs over its own data extent, rounded to nice ticks (not anchored at zero, so a measure of 95 to 99 fills its axis); no per-axis domain, and nulls break a line.
- **Table height**: rows that do not fit the chart's height are not drawn (no scroll or paging); `limit` cuts to the top N with no Other row.
- **Chord layout**: from-nodes on the left half, to-nodes on the right (label cuts are under Chord labels).
- **Marimekko values**: null and negative values count as 0; there is no percentage axis.
- **Waffle resolution**: 100 cells, so a share under 0.5% gets no cell (it still shows in the legend).
- **Radial labels**: category labels are cut at `max(4, floor(plot width * 0.2 / 7.2))` characters, about a fifth of the plot width, and thinned when they collide; a bar's value shows only at its tip, under the conditions of Radial tip values.
- **Parallel and limit**: the Other row that `limit` rolls up has no single line in a parallel chart, so it is not drawn.
- **Treemap slivers**: leaves under about 2 px a side are not drawn; their rows stay in the data table.
- **Flow label length**: sankey labels are cut at 20 characters, and one that would overlap its neighbour on the same side is dropped after a one-line try.
- **Link keyboard reach**: arrow keys walk sankey and chord nodes, not links; the hidden data table carries the same rows for screen readers.
- **Formatting speed path**: only en-US numbers with `maximumFractionDigits` and the built-in date presets skip `Intl`; any other locale or option set builds an `Intl` formatter once per page.
- **Formatter cache**: formatters are cached per locale and options for the page, and the cache is emptied when it passes 200 entries, not trimmed by use.
- **Label overlap scan**: in-chart value labels are checked against each other by 64 px column buckets (plain bars) or a full scan (a waterfall's two edge tags), which the 10000-mark cap bounds.
- **Legend for many series**: a multi-series legend needs the data, so the first paint of such a chart draws twice.
- **Host duplicate columns**: `columns()` reads the first of two same-named columns; each row's keys come out in reverse column order, which only shows in the unknown-field hint. `host.js` sits at its 810 B budget.
- **Version constant**: `version` is written by hand in `registry.ts` and a test fails when it differs from `package.json`. `MAJOR` keys the registry (`mayacharts.marks@0`); a pre-1.0 break of the Mark contract must bump it by hand, or two copies would share one table.
- **Beeswarm dodge**: each point checks only the 50 most recently placed neighbours, so a very dense tie cluster can overlap a little; the swarm is clamped to its row past about 25 points anyway.
- **Sunburst names**: a name runs straight along its arc, upright, cut so its chord stays inside the ring; it is never curved, and a slice too short for 4 characters has no name (the tooltip names it).
- **Drilled treemap tints**: a drilled treemap spreads its tiles' tints over the lightness range in size order, so neighbours in size differ only slightly when a branch holds many tiles.
- **Treemap ramp ink**: treemap tiles coloured by a `colorBy` ramp use dark ink on every step, so the darkest steps can fall under 4.5 to 1.
- **Sunburst of slivers**: a ring of hundreds of equal slices shows its largest as a thin slice and the rest as "Other (n)"; drill or filter to see them.
- **Sankey narrow labels**: a label slides only within its own node's height; small nodes crowded beside a bigger one lose their label (the tooltip still names them).
- **Hexmap hover ink**: while a hex is hovered, every label takes the text colour, so the hovered hex's own label can fall under 4.5 to 1.
- **Waterfall labels**: a step label wider than its column is dropped, with no thinning; the first bar and the Total are always labelled.
- **Roll-up bar**: the Other bar from `limit` never sets the value scale and is clipped at the plot edge, with no clip mark; its value is in the label and tooltip.
- **Radial year**: a shared trailing four-digit year moves to the centre caption, which hides below about 170 px.
- **Small heatmap cells**: cells under 26 px become squares with a 2 px gap, so a 52-week calendar at 360 px draws 3 px dots.
- **Line end labels**: drawn for 2 to 8 series; below 400 px a label is the last value alone in a value-wide gutter and the legend names the series (names would not fit). `endLabels: false` always uses the legend.
- **Gauge warn value**: printed beside its tick like the target label, but dropped (the tick stays) when it would touch the target label or leave the chart; the target label is clipped to the room left beside the dial.
- **Parallel end labels**: nudged apart only while they fit the plot height at 13 px each; with more lines a colliding name is dropped.
- **Bar value labels that would print over a taller neighbour are dropped, not thinned.
- **Sunburst ring names are cut with an ellipsis when the ring is too thin; the tooltip carries the full name.
- **Weave density**: under 32 px a period, only the first and last dots of a thread are full size (the rest are pinpricks); under 32 px a rank, dots are not labelled and the legend and tooltip name the threads.
- **Heatmap ink cuts**: heatmap and hexmap value ink switches at fixed ramp steps tuned for the default accent; a `colors` accent of a very different lightness can put a step under 4.5 to 1.
- **Weave caps at 8 series**: a weave throws `too-many-marks` above 8 threads, or 20 with a group `colorBy` (one per palette slot, groups share slots and `tint` tells threads apart); `limit` rolls up categories, never series, so filter the rows or fold the tail into one series first. Ranks are among the series present in a period, and tied values share a rank but keep separate rows in series order. A thread is named on the left axis (in first-period order, clipped to 16 characters), at the end, and above its first dot when it starts late or after a gap; that start name and `labels` values need rows 32 px apart. Below 32 px a step the middle dots shrink to pinpricks, and below 48 px the crossing halos are dropped. With no end gutter the last value sits right of its dot, and is dropped (the tooltip carries it) when it does not fit or rows are under 16 px apart.
- **Chord labels**: a label is cut to 36% of the plot width (20 characters at most) and dropped when its neighbour on the same side leaves no 16 px of height, so a narrow chart with many nodes names only the largest; the tooltip names the rest.
- **Ratio aggregate scope**: `aggregate: { ratio }` defines one measure (with a `y` array, keyed by y field, on types that toggle the measure, kpi only with `toggle`) on types whose values sit side by side; part-to-whole types (treemap, sunburst, sankey, chord, marimekko, waffle, funnel), stacks and `was` refuse it because ratios do not add up. Other fields (y2, tooltip fields, a colorBy field) sum.
- **Per-series style is dash and emphasis only**: `dash` names series drawn dashed and `emphasis` (line) series drawn heavier at one theme width (`lineEmphasis`); there is no per-series width or opacity map.
- **colorScale quantile**: five equal-count classes at fixed ramp steps; there is no class count option.
- **Diverging ramp from the spec only**: `rampMid` diverges the ramp when the spec's theme (tokens or a registered name) sets it; a `--maya-ramp-mid` set only in page CSS colours the middle but the steps stay sequential, since core places the values. `colorScale` other than linear keeps `rampMid` at the ramp's middle step, not at `rampMidValue`.
- **Stream offset**: `stack: "stream"` uses the wiggle baseline in series order (no inside-out reordering), takes values of 0 or more, and starts centred on 0 at the first category.
- **Theme geometry tokens**: `cellGap` and `cellRadius` are px read by the marks from the spec's theme, so a page-level `--maya-cell-gap` does not move cells.
- **Log scale on bars**: refused on purpose. A bar encodes its value as length from zero, and a log axis has no zero.
- **Sankey drill**: the drill starts from the first column, then continues one column at a time through any non-final node; a deeper middle node cannot be the entry point at the root, because its upstream is not one branch. The drilled node stays as the first column.
- **Drill leaf on flows**: sankey and chord fire `maya-drill-leaf` only once drilled to their last branch, where a node has a single path.
- **Title tokens**: `{max.x}` and the rest read the active measure per category of the drawn level (series combined), ignore hidden series and the zoom window, take no inline preset (`format` of the field applies) and read empty when there is no data.
- **Nested treemap depth**: `depth` draws that many levels below the current branch; the name strip of a parent costs 16 px of its height and is dropped when the parent is too small.

## What you can do instead

- **Custom formatters**: use `format` (string presets or `Intl.*Format` options) plus `titles` for display names and `labels: true` for on-mark values. Localisation via `locale` and `text`.
- **Time series in local time**: shift timestamps to the zone you want before charting, or pass Intl options with a `timeZone` in `format` for the labels.
- **Diverging bar chart**: use `colorBy: { target: 0 }` to tone positive and negative bars differently.
- **Complex drill UI**: listen to `maya-select` and `maya-view` events and control `el.view` and `el.selected` from your app.
- **Stacked negative bars**: supported (negatives stack below zero); for a running total use `waterfall`.
- **Facet and expand motion**: in WebKit a surviving facet panel jumps to its new cell (WebKit ignores CSS transform on a nested svg). A line drillTo has no zoom box and pops in. An opened list move and its evidence rows overlap for about 120 ms. Two thin drilled marimekko columns side by side can overprint their short names.
